Who We Are

A Community Grounded in Love & Grace

The core purpose of Souls Chapel is making Jesus known by sharing the Gospel, transforming lives through God’s Word, and serving the community by being an outreach church. We are focused on reaching the hopeless, feeding the hungry, and providing a place of worship for every person in the heart of Tallassee!

Our Purpose
Making Jesus known by sharing the Gospel, transforming lives through God’s Word, and serving the community by being an outreach church.
What We Believe
We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God. We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, and that the local church is called to display God's love to the world.
Our Leadership
Souls Chapel is led by elders and pastor Joshua Dixon. Our leadership structure is independent, focused on shepherd leadership, prayer, teaching, and serving the congregation.
